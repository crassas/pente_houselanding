Pentehouse, also written Pente House, is a barbershop in Marquês, Porto, open since 2018 at Rua da Constituição 656, Loja 20. We are near Praça do Marquês and Marquês Metro, with easy access from Faria Guimarães, Costa Cabral, Lindo Vale and Doze Casas. Haircuts, beard services and men’s grooming with Rascal, Grenha and Bruno Waya.
